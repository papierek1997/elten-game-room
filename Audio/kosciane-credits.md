# Dice General audio credits

The column cues repeat the score sound and append an effect when a bonus or school result is announced. The source effects were trimmed, adjusted in volume and combined with the score sound.

- School completion uses [Applause i.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Applause_i.ogg) by Thore, public domain.
- School failure uses [Sad Trombone.wav](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sad_Trombone.wav) by Sad Tromboner, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
