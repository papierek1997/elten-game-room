  Opening a creaking chest. Excerpt from 0.15 to 1.55 seconds of the

  and 100 ms closing fades. No extra musical or magical cue was added.
  Decoded duration: 1.40 seconds. Decoded sample peak: -3.93 dBFS.
  Downloaded source SHA-256:
  6a4b5758067803612f56749bb7943e4fa72e75f908639b1de50920300561463e
  Application asset SHA-256:
  d0a8bdb7184117cb3afce43226e3879fca4795c79b1dd5ff4b27d8e54d9cbb5e

Both effects were prepared as 48 kHz floating-point WAV excerpts with edge
fades and a sample-peak target of -4 dBFS, then encoded with the project's
tools/encode_audio.rb: Opus, 144 kbit/s VBR, audio mode, 20 ms frames,
complexity 10. The output preserves the selected source's channel count.
Source audio metadata was replaced with the title, author, source link,
licence and modification note above; these credits are also embedded in
the Opus files. The original downloaded files were not modified.

When distributing auction_chest.opus, retain the title, author, source,
licence link and modification notice. The adaptation remains under
CC BY 3.0; the application's code licence does not replace it.
