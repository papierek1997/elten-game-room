Billiards effects
=================

Downloaded from the public Freesound HQ previews on 12 November 2026.

Audio/billiards_collision.opus
  "billiard ball clack" by Za-Games (2020)
  Source: https://freesound.org/people/Za-Games/sounds/539854/
  Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/539/539854_12029332-hq.mp3
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Complete preview, original mono channel and level retained.

Audio/billiards_cue.opus
  Adaptation of that same CC0 clack, not a separate cue recording.
  Cue: 6500 Hz low-pass filter and gain 0.85.
  Duration and mono channel retained.

Audio/billiards_cushion.opus
  "pool-ball-bounce-off-rail.wav" by mccarthy@bedmas.com (2007)
  Source: https://freesound.org/people/mccarthy%40bedmas.com/sounds/42364/
  Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/42/42364_450724-hq.mp3
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  A real pool ball rebounding off a table rail. Complete preview retained,
  with its stereo channels; gain increased by 8 dB, no trimming.
  MP3 replay-gain metadata was removed when preparing the WAV intermediate.

Audio/billiards_pocket.opus
  "jf Pool Ball hit and pocket.wav" by cmusounddesign (2009)
  Source: https://freesound.org/people/cmusounddesign/sounds/85169/
  Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/85/85169_1059930-hq.mp3
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Full license: LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt
  Excerpt 1.15-3.10 seconds of the preview, retaining its stereo channels;
  10 ms opening and 100 ms closing fades. The recording contains both
  ball contact and pocket noise. No endorsement by the author is implied.

All four assets: Opus, 48 kHz, 144 kbit/s VBR, audio mode, 20 ms frames,
complexity 10, encoded with the project's tools/encode_audio.rb.
