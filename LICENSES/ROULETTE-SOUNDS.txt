# Roulette effects

Both source recordings are CC0 1.0 (public-domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The public high-quality MP3 previews were downloaded on 2026-11-08.

- `Audio/roulette_spin.opus`: Joao_Janz, *Roulette Wheel Spin Loop 1_1*,
  https://freesound.org/people/Joao_Janz/sounds/482663/ (2019-08-27).
  This is a synthesized layered roulette effect, not a field recording.
  Converted to 48 kHz stereo Opus, 144 kbit/s VBR, with a final 0.28-second fade.
- `Audio/roulette_chips.opus`: reg7783, *casino chips.wav*,
  https://freesound.org/people/reg7783/sounds/633997/ (2022-05-14).
  Clay casino chips handled and stacked. Excerpt from 1.50 to 2.15 seconds,
  converted to 48 kHz stereo Opus, 144 kbit/s VBR, with short edge fades.

No account, original-file download or paid sample library is required.
