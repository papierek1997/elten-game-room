Changes for Typoholism: initially selected 30,000 lowercase, 3–12-letter Polish word
forms, ranked by adjusted frequency (ARF), with dispersion 1-DP >= 0.05 and
explicit abbreviation/foreign-token exclusions. Paulinux's final manual review
removed 13 entries, leaving 29,987 forms, sorted and compressed for play.
The final list is preserved in tools/data/typing-polish-words.txt; it is not
automatically refilled. The historical selection and source/output checksums are in
content/mania_pisania_words_SOURCES.md and tools/data/typing-word-packs.json.
