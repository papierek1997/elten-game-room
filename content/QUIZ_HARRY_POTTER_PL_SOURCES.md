# Polish Harry Potter questions

`quiz.harry_potter.books.pl`, data version 3, contains 1,015 questions in eight
categories. Of LordKiller's 993 proposals, 985 were included. Power Games
reviewed their wording, correct answers and distractors individually. In the initial review,
241 included items were edited and 744 retained the submitted wording.
The set is in Polish regardless of the interface language.

Data version 2 addresses four reports from Zuzler: it supplies missing novel
or scene context, removes house and occupation clues from the prompts,
replaces two implausible distractors and corrects the source of the Polyjuice
disguise scene to *Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets*. The question
identities, correct answers and total count remain unchanged.

Data version 3 adds 30 questions based on 44 proposals from Zuzler. The
accepted items were checked against the seven Polish novels. Fourteen
proposals were excluded: they repeated existing or newly reviewed questions,
depended on an ill-defined count, or tested a made-up title. The earlier 985
questions retain their identities, wording and answers.

## Scope and verification

The scope is J.K. Rowling's seven Harry Potter novels, from *Harry Potter
and the Philosopher's Stone* through *Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows*.
The review used the supplied Polish texts translated by Andrzej Polkowski.
Each retained question names the relevant literary source; selected details
were corroborated with the official HarryPotter.com encyclopedia or features.
Film-only scenes and supplementary biographies were not silently treated as
events from the novels. The questions contain spoilers throughout the series.

The supplied text copies contain OCR or edition discrepancies. In particular,
the accepted values of 29 Knuts to a Sickle, 142 Hogwarts staircases and an
Ollivanders founding date of 382 BCE were checked independently against the
official site rather than copying conflicting digits from those files:

- [Wizarding money](https://www.harrypotter.com/fact-file/objects/wizarding-money).
- [Hogwarts facts](https://www.harrypotter.com/features/10-cool-hogwarts-facts-to-impress-your-friends).
- [Ollivanders](https://www.harrypotter.com/fact-file/locations/ollivanders).

The source novels, attachments and private verification excerpts are not
distributed with the application. The `medium` difficulty label is an
editorial default, not a measurement from player testing.

## Exclusions

Question IDs retain their original proposal positions, so these gaps are
intentional. Excluded material has not been replaced with unrelated filler.

- 97 repeats item 30: the identity of Moody's impersonator.
- 559 asks about Arthur's rubber-duck question, a film scene rather than
  the first conversation at the Burrow in the novels.
- 839 repeats item 354: Bill and Fleur's home, Shell Cottage.
- 438, 836, 954, 955 and 982 were withheld because their exact details were
  not sufficiently established in the supplied literary texts: Harry's vault
  number, the twins' precise birthday, Quirrell's previous teaching post,
  the location of his first meeting with Voldemort, and James Potter's
  Head Boy role as described by Hagrid. This is a limit of this review,
  not a claim that all these details are false in the wider canon. For
  item 982 the supplied Polish passage praises Harry's parents without
  expressly naming the school roles.
