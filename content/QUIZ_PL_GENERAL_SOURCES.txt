Polish general knowledge — sources and rights (data version 19)

This set replaces the earlier Wikidata-based Polish general set. Its legacy
pack and set identifiers remain unchanged so that the existing Polish general
selection still works. The question database, checksum and data version change.

assistance for Power Games. The original dataset and programme authors did not
write the added distractors and do not endorse this adaptation.

Data version 8 minimally revises 410 prompts with embedded alternatives. It
keeps all 14,887 questions, their order and categories. Answer lists are removed
without deleting factual clues; some answer forms are adjusted for grammar or
to keep the meaning of a comparison after removing the three-choice restriction.
The readable review lists are generated from the runtime data with
tools/export-quiz-text.rb and are not duplicated inside the installer.
