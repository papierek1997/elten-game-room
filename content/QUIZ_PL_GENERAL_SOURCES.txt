Polish general knowledge — sources and rights (data version 9)

This set replaces the earlier Wikidata-based Polish general set. Its legacy
pack and set identifiers remain unchanged so that the existing Polish general
selection still works. The question database, checksum and data version change.

Adaptation: selection for four-choice play, categories and three additional
answer choices. Confirmed answer errors were corrected after reviewing flagged
records; questions whose faulty premises would require rewriting were excluded.
Original question wording is preserved apart from approved minimal edits to
remove embedded answer lists and make the prompt fit four-choice play. Necessary
grammatical adjustments and dependent answer choices are recorded with the edits.
The unmodified earlier export and the before/after editorial decisions are
retained separately from the runtime pack.
Data version 9 writes centuries with Arabic ordinal numerals followed by a
period (for example "17. wieku" instead of "XVII wieku"); regnal numbers and
proper names keep their Roman numerals.

The source contains automatically transcribed television quiz questions.
Adaptation: selection, duplicate removal, clear spelling/transcription repairs,
verified answer keys, categories and three additional answer choices.
Unclear transcriptions and inherently two-choice questions are excluded.

Milionerzy
Source: the public question archive at https://www.polsat.pl/.
Each retained question stores its individual source page URL. Original answer
choices are retained except for explicit editorial corrections.

Editorial adaptation and additional answer choices were prepared with AI
assistance for Power Games. The original dataset and programme authors did not
write the added distractors and do not endorse this adaptation.
Each question retains its source ID, source_dataset, source_license and URL.
Data version 8 minimally revises 410 prompts with embedded alternatives. It
keeps all 14,887 questions, their order and categories. Answer lists are removed
without deleting factual clues; some answer forms are adjusted for grammar or
to keep the meaning of a comparison after removing the three-choice restriction.
The readable review lists are generated from the runtime data with
tools/export-quiz-text.rb and are not duplicated inside the installer.
