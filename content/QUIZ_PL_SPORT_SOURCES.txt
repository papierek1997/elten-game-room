Polish Quiz Party sport question provenance

Reviewed on 2026-09-13.

Removed coach questions

The 131 present-tense questions of the form "Which club does this coach manage?" were removed. Wikidata property P6087 includes assistant and staff roles as well as head coaches, and many statements lack dates or references. Adding a year to those records would still produce misleading questions.

Reviewed club founding years

Twenty-six replacement questions use a club founding year that was checked against the reference attached to its Wikidata P571 statement and an independent reputable or official source. Ambiguous legal-incorporation, merger, predecessor, multisport-club and football-section dates were rejected. Every accepted question stores both the Wikidata entity URL and the cited reference URL.
