Russian general knowledge — provenance and editorial review

The original 36 questions, version 1, are an original ELTEN Game Room editorial
set released under CC0-1.0. They are retained unchanged in version 2.

On November 16, 2026, monstricek submitted 37 questions through the Power Games
question creator for the Russian general-knowledge set. All items and answer
choices were reviewed individually on November 17. Thirty-one proposals were
accepted after editing; six were left out. They are community submissions for
Power Games, not part of the original CC0 grant. The set's metadata identifies
both portions rather than relicensing the submitted wording. The attachment
and private correspondence are not application resources.

The 31 additions have stable IDs monstricek_20261116_03 through
monstricek_20261116_37, with gaps for excluded proposals 01, 02, 17, 21, 28 and
31. Each accepted question has its own source reference in the data. The
research sources confirm facts; their text is not imported as a question bank.

Editorial changes
- Replace literal, joke and overlapping distractors with distinct alternatives.
- Ask about the three concrete meanings of "кисть", not whether it is the only
  polysemous word among several polysemous plant names.
- Ask about Greek mythology, not an unverified direct Greek-language loan.
- Separate the Baltic Spit's name from its synonymous Vistula/Frische Nehrung
  names: those are not three different geographical answers.
- Identify Kuibyshev by its former name Kainsk, so the former Samara name does
  not make the question ambiguous.
- Date the time-zone reform and diamond-production question. Population
  superlatives are checked against the cities' own public information.
- Distinguish Magellan's initial command in 1519 from Elcano's completion of
  the voyage. Ask about the surrender of Paulus's surrounded army instead of
  the vague "turning point", which could also suggest the Battle of Kursk.
- Keep only one correct answer, natural Russian phrasing and normal ё spelling.

Excluded proposals
01: Correctly spelled choices disclose which one contains the requested letter;
    the item tests reading the answer rather than the intended spelling rule.
02: The supplied punctuation key is doubtful and the alternatives mix comma
    counts with an unrelated colon instruction. Not added as submitted.
17: "A phraseological unit having only singular form" does not define a clear,
    common grammatical comparison among the four proposed expressions.
21: The proposed Chinese land/sea-border key is not substantiated; the other
    choices also mix distinct maritime-border situations. Not added.
28: The unqualified "oldest continuously inhabited settlement in Russia"
    combines disputed age, continuity and territorial scope. Not added.
31: The attribution mixes conservation of energy, Leibniz's vis viva and the
    work–kinetic-energy theorem. It is not safe as a single-answer question.

Review scope: 37 proposals, all four choices in each, comparison with all
36 existing questions, separate language read-through and technical validation.
The accepted set contains 67 questions. No English/Polish content was translated
to fill gaps, and no new game rule or interface translation is introduced.
